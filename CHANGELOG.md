# Changelog

Notable changes by release. Releases are git tags `vX.Y.Z`. New work goes under Unreleased.

## Unreleased

- Cargo workspace with the core, nn, facade, datasets and bench crates, and shared lints.
- `Real`, `Shape`, `Tensor` (with views) and `Error` in `cavitation-core`.
- `SplitMix64` and `Rng` (xoshiro256++) in `cavitation-core`.
- Strided matrix views (`MatRef`, `MatMut`) and reference `gemm` and `matvec` kernels in `cavitation-core`.
- Reverse-mode autograd in `cavitation-core`: `Graph`, `Tape` and `Var`, the `ParamStore` for parameters and their gradients, and the first operation, `add`.
- `gradcheck` and `gradcheck_params`: a numerical gradient check in `f64` (central differences) with a report of every element outside the tolerance.
- `sub`, `mul` and `scale` on the tape, and NumPy-style broadcasting for `add`, `sub` and `mul` (with a fast path for the bias add).
- `matmul` and `matmul_nt` on the tape, with gradients computed on strided views, and `Real::gemm`.
- `sum`, `sum_axis`, `mean`, `mean_axis`, `relu`, `leaky_relu`, `tanh`, `sigmoid` and `gelu` (exact and tanh forms, `GeluApprox`) on the tape, and `Real::erf` (ported from FreeBSD msun).
- `elu`, `selu`, `celu`, `silu`, `mish`, `softplus`, `softsign`, `hardtanh`, `relu6`, `hardsigmoid`, `hardswish`, `log_sigmoid` and `prelu` on the tape, following PyTorch's definitions and boundary conventions; `relu` and the clamp family now pass NaN through instead of turning it into 0.
- `glu` on the tape with `Gate`: GLU (sigmoid, as PyTorch `F.glu`), GeGLU, ReGLU and SwiGLU, splitting the last axis; NaN passes through every gate.
- `softmax`, `log_softmax`, `softmax_cross_entropy` and `mse` on the tape, with `Reduction` (`Mean`, `Sum`, `None`, `BatchMean`); cross-entropy is fused, uses the log-sum-exp form and gives a NaN loss for NaN logits.
- `binary_cross_entropy` (with a count of clamped logarithms, `Tape::clamped_logs`), `binary_cross_entropy_with_logits`, `nll`, `l1`, `huber`, `smooth_l1` and `kl_div` on the tape, following PyTorch's definitions, each taking a `Reduction`; NaN input gives a NaN loss.
- User-defined operations: the `CustomOp` trait (forward, backward and output shape, generic over `Real` so `gradcheck` runs on it in `f64`) and `Tape::custom`, taking up to 4 inputs and allocating nothing after warm-up.
- PyTorch fixtures for every tape operation (generated by `scripts/fixtures/make_fixtures.py`, PyTorch 2.15.0 CPU) and a test that compares outputs and input gradients with them.
