# Changelog

Notable changes by release. Releases are git tags `vX.Y.Z`. New work goes under Unreleased.

## Unreleased

- Cargo workspace with the core, nn, facade, datasets and bench crates, and shared lints.
- `Real`, `Shape`, `Tensor` (with views) and `Error` in `cavitation-core`.
- `SplitMix64` and `Rng` (xoshiro256++) in `cavitation-core`.
- Strided matrix views (`MatRef`, `MatMut`) and reference `gemm` and `matvec` kernels in `cavitation-core`.
- Reverse-mode autograd in `cavitation-core`: `Graph`, `Tape` and `Var`, the `ParamStore` for parameters and their gradients, and the first operation, `add`.
- `gradcheck` and `gradcheck_params`: a numerical gradient check in `f64` (central differences) with a report of every element outside the tolerance.
- `sub`, `mul` and `scale` on the tape, and NumPy-style broadcasting for `add`, `sub` and `mul` (with a fast path for the bias add).
- `matmul` and `matmul_nt` on the tape, with gradients computed on strided views, and `Real::gemm`.
- `sum`, `sum_axis`, `mean`, `mean_axis`, `relu`, `leaky_relu`, `tanh`, `sigmoid` and `gelu` (exact and tanh forms, `GeluApprox`) on the tape, and `Real::erf` (ported from FreeBSD msun).
- `elu`, `selu`, `celu`, `silu`, `mish`, `softplus`, `softsign`, `hardtanh`, `relu6`, `hardsigmoid`, `hardswish`, `log_sigmoid` and `prelu` on the tape, following PyTorch's definitions and boundary conventions; `relu` and the clamp family now pass NaN through instead of turning it into 0.
- `glu` on the tape with `Gate`: GLU (sigmoid, as PyTorch `F.glu`), GeGLU, ReGLU and SwiGLU, splitting the last axis; NaN passes through every gate.
- `softmax`, `log_softmax`, `softmax_cross_entropy` and `mse` on the tape, with `Reduction` (`Mean`, `Sum`, `None`, `BatchMean`); cross-entropy is fused, uses the log-sum-exp form and gives a NaN loss for NaN logits.
- `binary_cross_entropy` (with a count of clamped logarithms, `Tape::clamped_logs`), `binary_cross_entropy_with_logits`, `nll`, `l1`, `huber`, `smooth_l1` and `kl_div` on the tape, following PyTorch's definitions, each taking a `Reduction`; NaN input gives a NaN loss.
- User-defined operations: the `CustomOp` trait (forward, backward and output shape, generic over `Real` so `gradcheck` runs on it in `f64`) and `Tape::custom`, taking up to 4 inputs and allocating nothing after warm-up.
- PyTorch fixtures for every tape operation (generated by `scripts/fixtures/make_fixtures.py`, PyTorch 2.15.0 CPU) and a test that compares outputs and input gradients with them.
- Kernel levels (`Level`: reference, SSE4.2, AVX2 with FMA, NEON) detected at run time, with `set_level` and the `CAVITATION_KERNEL` override; the thread count (`threads`, `set_threads`, `CAVITATION_THREADS`); and a persistent thread pool, `kernels::pool::run`, that allocates nothing per call, runs nested and concurrent calls inline, and passes task panics back to the caller.
- The packed f32 GEMM, `kernels::gemm::gemm_f32` (and `gemm_f32_with` for an explicit level and thread count): Goto-style blocking and packing with an SSE4.2 microkernel, split across the thread pool with bit-identical results for any thread count, allocating nothing after warm-up; `Real::gemm` for f32 now uses it. `cavitation-bench gemm` times the matrix products of a training step.
- AVX2 with FMA (6 by 16 tiles) and NEON (8 by 12 tiles) GEMM microkernels: `Level::Avx2Fma` and `Level::Neon` now run their own packed kernels, chosen at run time, instead of the SSE4.2 kernel and the reference loops.
- The PyTorch fixture comparisons now run on every kernel level this CPU supports, and a test fails for any fixture whose operation no comparison covers.
- `Init` (Xavier and He initialisers, zeros, constant), the object-safe `Layer` trait and `Linear` (weight `[outputs, inputs]` like PyTorch) in `cavitation-nn`.
- `Activation`, `ActivationKind`, `ActivationLayer` (user-defined activations run as a custom operation built once), a layer for every built-in activation, `Softmax`, `LogSoftmax`, `Glu`, `Prelu`, and `Sequential` with `Trace` and PyTorch-style parameter names (`0.weight`, `2.bias`) in `cavitation-nn`; `Sequential` checks the width between layers and names the layer when it does not chain.
- `Optimizer` and `GradStats`, with `Sgd` (momentum, weight decay), `Adam` and `AdamW`, matching `torch.optim` over ten-step trajectories, in `cavitation-nn`; the optimiser collects each gradient's norm and finiteness in the same pass as the update.
- `Targets`, `Dataset`, `Batch`, `BatchView` and `Batcher` in `cavitation-nn` (shuffled, allocation-free batches whose smaller last batch reuses the full-size buffer), `TensorView::from_slice`, the scikit-learn digits data in `data/digits/`, the digits and MNIST loaders in `cavitation-datasets`, and `scripts/data/get-mnist.sh`.
- Training in `cavitation-nn`: `Trainer` (one step: forward, loss, backward, optimiser step, allocation-free after a warm-up epoch), `Loss` (cross-entropy, NLL, MSE, L1, Huber, smooth L1, BCE, BCE with logits, KL divergence), `fit` with `FitConfig`, `OptimizerConfig`, `History` and `EpochReport`, and `accuracy`; known-result tests for XOR and the scikit-learn digits (97.5% on the held-out 360).
- Forward and backward hooks in `cavitation-nn`: `Hooks` (with `HookId`) runs closures on chosen layers after each forward or backward pass, each seeing a `LayerEvent` with the layer's input, output, local derivative (f′(z) for an activation, 1 or the slope for `Prelu`) and the gradients with respect to its output and input; `Trainer::step_with_hooks` and `fit_with_hooks` run them, allocation-free after warm-up. `Layer::local_derivative` (an element-wise layer's derivative, by default from its `Activation`) and `Tape::param_value`.
